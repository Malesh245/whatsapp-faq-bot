## About us
Reliable Paper Mart sells paper and stationery to homes, schools and offices.
WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us.

## Store timings
- Monday to Saturday: 9:30 AM to 7:30 PM
- Sunday: Closed
- Public holidays: Closed

## Products and prices (prices include GST)
- A4 copier paper, 75 GSM, ream of 500 sheets: ₹260
- A4 copier paper, 80 GSM, ream of 500 sheets: ₹290
- A3 copier paper, 75 GSM, ream of 500 sheets: ₹540 (A3 is double the size of A4)
- Legal size paper, 75 GSM, ream of 500 sheets: ₹320
- A4 paper box (5 reams of 75 GSM): ₹1,250
- Chart paper, pack of 25 sheets: ₹150
- Spiral notebook, A5, 200 pages: ₹60
- Spiral notebook, A4, 200 pages: ₹95
- Brown envelopes, A4 size, pack of 50: ₹120

## Bulk orders
- Orders of 10 reams or more get 5% off the listed price.

## Ordering
- Send the product name, quantity and delivery address on WhatsApp. Our team confirms the order and total.
- Minimum order for delivery: ₹500.

## Delivery
- Free delivery within the city on orders above ₹1,000; otherwise the delivery charge is ₹50.
- Orders confirmed before 2 PM are delivered the same day within the city.
- Outside the city we ship by courier in 3 to 5 working days.

## Payment
- We accept UPI, cash, debit/credit cards and bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS).
- GST invoices are available for businesses.

## Returns and replacement
- Unopened products can be returned within 7 days with the bill.
- Products damaged in delivery must be reported within 24 hours for a free replacement.
